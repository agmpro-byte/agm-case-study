The work got done. Nobody asked for the money.

At Heavenly Greens, the record that says a job is finished and the record that says it has been paid live in different places. When the two don’t meet, a finished job can simply sit there. Nobody was hiding anything. No single system could see it.

What one list found

We made one list of every finished job that hadn’t been paid in full. The first time we ran it, at the end of October 2026, it found about $80,000 owed to the company.

On November 4, the system’s own count showed 41 finished jobs not paid in full. The oldest had been waiting 581 days.

Those are two separate counts, taken a few days apart. The 41 jobs are not a breakdown of the $80,000, so we don’t add them up or split them.

What changed

The list now keeps every finished job in view until it is paid. A job joins it when the work is finished and leaves only when it is paid in full. Nothing ages out of sight.

What we can’t tell you yet

How much of that money has come in. We haven’t measured it yet, and we won’t guess. When there’s a real number, it goes on this page.

Build this list yourself, this week

You don’t need our software for this. Any CRM or spreadsheet will do:

  1. List every job that is finished but not paid in full.
  2. Next to each one, note the date it finished and what is still owed.
  3. Sort by the oldest first, and look at it every week.

Source: the $80,000 is Troy Scott’s figure from October 31, 2026. The 41 jobs and the 581 days are the system’s own count from November 4, 2026. No collected-dollars result has been measured yet.
